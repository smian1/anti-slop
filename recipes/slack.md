# Recipe: Slack

Short, blunt, human. Preserve the writer's edge.

## Defaults

- **Edit** or **Draft** with maximum voice keep.
- No em dashes. Parentheses OK.
- Sparse fragments OK; don't manufacture staccato drama stacks.
- Contractions fine. Profanity/edge fine when they'd say it.
- No markdown headers, emoji bullets, or bold-everywhere in casual channels.
- No "I hope this helps!" / "Great question!" residue.
- One idea → short paragraph. Lists only for genuinely parallel items (≤5).

## Cuts that matter most here

Throat-clearing, binary contrasts, puffery, false agency, chatbot politeness, decorative formatting.

## Keep

Blunt judgments, humor, uncertainty markers (*actually*, *honestly*, *I think*), unfinished thoughts that match how they type.

## Don't

- Professionalize a blunt line into corp-speak.
- Inject soul the message didn't ask for.
- Expand a three-line Slack into an essay.
- Invent owners, ETAs, or promises in incident/support threads → switch to Evidence-bound.
