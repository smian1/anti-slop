# Recipe: social (tweets, LinkedIn, short bios)

Short-form rules + Draft mode. Load `references/banned-words.md`.

## Formatting

- No markdown headers in casual posts.
- No bold random phrases for emphasis.
- No emoji-as-bullet stacks (✅🔥 every line). One or two emoji per post max if they'd actually use them.
- No "🧵" / "Thread:" openers — earn the read.
- Hashtags: 0–2, natural — no stacks.
- No markdown in plain-text DMs/SMS (raw asterisks = tell).

## Structure

- **No default rule of three.** Use the true count.
- **Vary sentence length.** No three consecutive same-length sentences.
- **Anti-parataxis for anything longer than a tweet:** short. short. short. is itself a tell. Connect with clauses, conjunctions, semicolons.
  - Slack one-liners may stay punchy; threads and LinkedIn posts should connect thoughts.
- Em dashes: max ~1 per 500 words (prefer zero). Parentheses/semicolons encouraged.
- Exclamation marks: rare.
- Pick a side; one-sentence counterpoint max — no hedging seesaw.

## Honesty

- Never invent anecdotes, stats, or quotes. Hypotheticals labeled (*imagine…*).
- Name real things. Use numbers you have.
- Include friction/doubt when true — not corporate pep talk.

## Voice calibration

Match the person: slang, swearing, length, humor. Default if unknown: direct, slightly informal, contractions, may start with And/But, trusts the reader.

## Silent self-check

Banned words? Same-length streak? Parataxis stack? Threes? Hedging? Extra em dashes? Fabricated specifics? Could any AI have written this for any person? → add something real or cut.
