# Recipe: Marketing landing

Sell with the product's real story. Do not paste the AI SaaS template.

## Defaults

- **Edit** or **Draft** with DESIGN.md / brand first.
- Sections earn their place from content. No testimonials/logos/FAQ without material.
- One primary CTA; secondary optional. No purple CTA by default.
- Hierarchy via type size/weight/space — not glass cards.

## Cuts that matter most here

Purple/indigo/cyan–pink hero wash; mesh orbs; gradient text; Inter monoculture; three identical feature cards; eyebrow spam; emoji icons; fake logo bar; hero→3 features→FAQ→purple CTA as autopilot; cream+terracotta kit as "premium" default; bounce/pulse loops.

## Keep / prefer

- Real product screenshot or demo over div fake chrome
- Flagship feature treated differently from supporting points
- Authored section rhythm (vary layout when content differs)
- Honest proof (named customers, real metrics) or none
- `prefers-reduced-motion`; one entrance max

## Don't

- Invent social proof, metrics, or Jane Doe quotes
- Replace purple slop with beige-serif slop
- Force bento/pricing-three-up when the offer is not that shape
- Always-on sparkle badges ("AI Powered")
