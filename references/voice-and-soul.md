# Voice and soul

When to preserve vs inject. Sample overrides generic style rules.

## Preserve first

Notice vocabulary, cadence, bluntness, humor, uncertainty, digressions, polish level. Keep traits that feel like the writer. Do not make every paragraph equally tidy. Rough drafts with real voice should still sound like the same person after Edit.

Keep edge, dry humor, blunt lines, honest uncertainty (*I think / maybe / to be honest*). Do not professionalize or sand down.

## Sample override

If a writing sample is present:

- Match em-dash rate (may restore dashes in long essays).
- Match fragment tolerance, formality, swearing, first-person rate.
- Do not inject slang, jokes, typos, or confessions the sample does not support.

## Soul — Draft only, light

Sterile, voiceless writing is also an AI tell. In **Draft** (not Edit, not org/legal evidence-bound):

- Have opinions; react instead of neutral pro/con lists.
- Vary rhythm: short next to long.
- Acknowledge complexity ("impressive and also unsettling").
- Use *I* when it fits.
- Let some mess in; perfect structure looks machine-made.
- Be specific with scenes/times when sourced — not fabricated anecdotes.

In **Edit**: do not inject personality the draft didn't have. Surgical phrasing only.

In **Org / legal / incident / support**: no soul injection. Evidence-bound + plain speech.

## Optional voice profiles (not default)

`casual | professional | technical | warm | blunt` — use only if the user asks. Default is match-the-writer, not pick-a-profile.

## Anti-patterns when "humanizing"

- Detector-gaming (unicode tricks, random typos) — forbidden.
- Replacing slop with a new formula (aphorism stacks, tidy triads).
- Flattening non-native or neurodivergent cadence into generic "clear" prose.
- Inventing personal stories for specificity.

## Fragments vs connected prose

| Venue | Preference |
|-------|------------|
| Slack / short Draft | Sparse fragments OK |
| Long essays / org posts | Prefer connected syntax; flag staccato drama stacks |
| Detect | One earned fragment ≠ slop; stacks of punchy one-liners = slop |
